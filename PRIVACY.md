# Privacy Policy: Keyboard Layout Fixer (EN ⇄ FA)

Keyboard Layout Fixer does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- **Your text:** All conversion happens on your device, inside your browser. Text is only read from a text box when you choose "Fix keyboard layout" from the right-click menu, and it is never sent anywhere.
- **Settings:** The only thing saved is your keyboard layout choice ("Persian" or "Persian (Standard)"), using your browser's own extension storage. If browser sync is turned on, your browser may sync this setting between your devices.
- **Network:** The extension makes no network requests.
- **Third parties:** No data is sold or shared with anyone.
